Create Your Irresistible Offer

Build an offer so good people feel stupid saying no. Learn value-based pricing and offer stacking.

Alex Hormozi's Rule:

"Make an offer so good people feel stupid saying no."

The Offer is Everything

You can have the best product in the world. But if your offer sucks, you won't sell anything.

Here's what most people get wrong:

Bad Offer: "I'll help you grow your business. $500/month."

Why it sucks: Vague, no specific outcome, no urgency, no risk reversal.

Good Offer: "Get your first 10 paying customers in 30 days or your money back. Includes 3 done-for-you outreach campaigns, daily accountability check-ins, and proven scripts. Normally $2,000, this week only $997. Only 5 spots available."

Why it works: Specific outcome, timeline, risk reversal, scarcity, value stack.

The Value Equation

Perceived Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort)

↑ INCREASE THESE:

  • • Dream Outcome: Make the result irresistible
  • • Perceived Likelihood: Prove it works (testimonials, guarantees)

↓ DECREASE THESE:

  • • Time Delay: Get them results FAST
  • • Effort: Make it easy to implement

The Offer Stack Formula

Don't sell one thing. Sell a STACK of value.

The 7 Components of an Irresistible Offer:

1. Core Offer (The Thing)

Example: "30-Day Customer Acquisition Sprint"

2. Specific Outcome (The Promise)

Example: "Get your first 10 paying customers in 30 days"

3. Timeline (Speed)

Example: "In 30 days" (not "eventually")

4. What's Included (The Stack)

Example: "3 done-for-you campaigns + daily check-ins + proven scripts"

5. Bonuses (Overdeliver)

Example: "BONUS: 50 email templates ($297 value)"

6. Guarantee (Risk Reversal)

Example: "Get 10 customers or full refund"

7. Scarcity/Urgency (Why Now)

Example: "Only 5 spots available / Ends Friday"

Pricing: The $100K Mistake

Most entrepreneurs leave $100K+ on the table by underpricing.

The Pricing Rule:

Charge 10x what you think you should. Then cut it in half. That's your real price.

Value-Based Pricing

Price based on the VALUE you create, not your time.

Example: If you help someone make an extra $50K, charging $10K (20% of value) is a no-brainer for them.

The Anchor Effect

Always show a higher "original price" first.

Example: "Normally $2,000 → Today only $997" (50% off feels like a steal)

The 3-Tier Strategy

Offer Good / Better / Best pricing.

Example:

  • Basic: $497 (DIY course)
  • Pro: $1,497 (Course + templates + group coaching) ← MOST POPULAR
  • Elite: $4,997 (Everything + 1-on-1 coaching)

Testing Your Offer

The 10-Person Test:

Pitch your offer to 10 people in your target market.

  • • If 8+ say "yes" → Your price is too low
  • • If 3-5 say "yes" → Perfect, you're in the sweet spot
  • • If 0-2 say "yes" → Price too high OR offer unclear
