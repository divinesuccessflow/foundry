About FOUNDRY

Building the future of entrepreneurship, one founder at a time.

Our Mission

Make entrepreneurship accessible to everyone by providing free, proven frameworks that eliminate confusion and accelerate success.

The Story

FOUNDRY was born from frustration.

Yashaswi, the founder, watched countless aspiring entrepreneurs drown in a sea of contradictory advice. They bought course after course. Read book after book. Watched video after video. But never launched.

The problem wasn't lack of information—it was too much information. No clarity. No simple path forward. Just gurus selling dreams and leaving people more confused than when they started.

So he built FOUNDRY: a platform that gives away the blueprints for free. No fluff. No hype. Just proven frameworks, templates, and tools that work.

The Philosophy:

"Give away the blueprints. Charge for the building."

We believe in abundance over scarcity. When you freely share knowledge, it comes back multiplied. The best marketing is making your customers successful.

Rama Rajya: Every Person an Entrepreneur

The ancient Indian concept of Rama Rajya describes an ideal society where prosperity, justice, and freedom are abundant for all.

We believe the modern path to this vision is through entrepreneurship—not as a privilege for the few, but as an accessible opportunity for everyone.

🌟

Abundance

There's enough for everyone. Your success doesn't diminish mine. We rise together.

⚖️

Justice

Access to knowledge shouldn't be gatekept. Everyone deserves the tools to build their future.

🕊️

Freedom

True freedom comes from economic independence. Entrepreneurship is the path.

When every person has the knowledge and tools to build their own enterprise, we create a society of empowered individuals. That's Rama Rajya in the modern age.

About Yashaswi

Yashaswi is the founder of Divine Success Flow, a movement dedicated to making entrepreneurship accessible, ethical, and abundant.

After years of building businesses, creating AI agents, and coaching entrepreneurs, he realized the same patterns repeated everywhere: people overwhelmed by information but starved for clarity.

FOUNDRY is his answer—a platform that strips away the noise and gives you exactly what you need to launch and grow.
