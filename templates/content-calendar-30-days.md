## 🎯 Content Strategy Philosophy

**The 70-20-10 Rule:**
- 70% Educational (teach, inform, add value)
- 20% Entertaining (stories, behind-the-scenes, personality)
- 10% Promotional (sell, pitch, CTA)

**The 4 Content Pillars:**
Every piece of content should fit into one of these:
1. **Educate** - Teach something valuable
2. **Inspire** - Share stories, wins, transformations
3. **Entertain** - Be human, show personality, build connection
4. **Promote** - Showcase your products/services

## 🛠️ CONTENT CREATION WORKFLOW

### Daily Routine (60-90 minutes total):
1. **Morning (15 min):** Check engagement, reply to comments
2. **Mid-day (45-60 min):** Create today's content
3. **Evening (15 min):** Schedule tomorrow's content, engage

### Batching Strategy (Save time):
- **Write:** Batch 4-7 blog posts on Monday
- **Film:** Batch 4-7 videos on Wednesday
- **Design:** Batch graphics on Friday
- **Schedule:** Use Buffer, Later, or Hootsuite

## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Repurpose everything** - 1 blog post = 1 video + 5 tweets + 3 Instagram posts
2. **Use templates** - Don't start from scratch every time
3. **Batch create** - Film 7 videos in one session
4. **Schedule ahead** - Use Buffer, Later, or CoSchedule
5. **Engage daily** - Content without engagement = shouting into the void
6. **Analyze monthly** - What performed best? Do more of that.
7. **Adjust as you go** - This isn't set in stone. Pivot based on what works.
