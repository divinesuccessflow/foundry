# Email Sequence Templates

**5 Proven Email Sequences That Convert**

---

## 📧 Why Email Sequences Matter

**The stats:**
- Email ROI: $42 for every $1 spent (4,200% ROI)
- Automated emails get 70.5% higher open rates than broadcast emails
- Sequence emails convert 3-5x better than one-off emails

**The psychology:**
- People need 7-12 touchpoints before buying

- Automation = you make money while you sleep

## 🔄 SEQUENCE #4: RE-ENGAGEMENT SEQUENCE

**Goal:** Wake up inactive subscribers, segment your list

**Timing:** 3 emails over 7 days

---

### EMAIL 1: "Are you still there?" (Day 1)

**Subject:** "Did I lose you?"

**Body:**
```
[First Name],

I noticed you haven't opened my emails in a while.

That's okay. Life happens.

But I want to make sure I'm still adding value.

So quick question:

**Are you still interested in [your topic]?**

If YES → Just click here: [LINK] (I'll know you're still engaged)

If NO → No hard feelings. Unsubscribe here: [LINK]

Either way, I appreciate you.

[Your Name]
```

---

### EMAIL 2: "Last chance to stay" (Day 4)

**Subject:** "Should I remove you from my list?"

**Body:**
```
[First Name],

I'm cleaning up my email list.

If you want to stay subscribed and keep getting [value you provide], 
click here: [LINK]

If I don't hear from you in the next 3 days, I'll assume you're not 
interested and remove you from the list.

No hard feelings either way.

[Your Name]
```

---

### EMAIL 3: "Goodbye" (Day 7)

**Subject:** "This is goodbye..."

**Body:**
```
[First Name],

I haven't heard from you, so I'm going to remove you from my list.

Before I do, I wanted to say thanks for being a subscriber—even if 
it didn't work out.

If you change your mind, you can always re-subscribe here: [LINK]

Otherwise, best of luck with [their goal].

[Your Name]

P.S. If you're still interested but just haven't had time to engage, 
click here to stay on the list: [LAST CHANCE LINK]
```
