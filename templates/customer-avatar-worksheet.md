# Customer Avatar Worksheet

**Get crystal clear on who you're serving**

---

## 🎯 Why This Matters

"If you're marketing to everyone, you're marketing to no one."

The more specific you are about WHO you serve, the easier it is to:
- Write copy that converts
- Create products they actually want
- Find where they hang out
- Speak their language
- Solve their real problems

## 💡 HOW TO USE THIS AVATAR

### 1. **Writing Copy:**
Use their language, speak to their pain points, show the transformation they want.

**Bad:** "Our business templates help entrepreneurs."

**Good:** "Stop drowning in conflicting advice. Get the exact step-by-step frameworks you need to launch in 30 days—no fluff, no overwhelm."

### 2. **Creating Products:**
Build solutions for their specific problems, not generic advice.

**Bad:** "General business course"

**Good:** "30-Day Consulting Launch Blueprint: Everything you need to land your first 3 clients"

### 3. **Choosing Channels:**
Go where they already hang out.

Sarah is on Instagram, LinkedIn, and YouTube → focus there, not TikTok or Facebook.

### 4. **Pricing:**
Match their budget and buying behavior.

Sarah budgets $200-$500 and is research-heavy → offer free value first, then $47-$297 products with testimonials.

## ✅ VALIDATION CHECKLIST

Don't guess—validate your avatar:

- [ ] **Talk to 5-10 real people** who match this profile
- [ ] **Read reviews** of competitors' products (what do they complain about?)
- [ ] **Join their communities** (forums, Facebook groups, subreddits)
- [ ] **Analyze comments** on YouTube, Instagram, blog posts
- [ ] **Send surveys** to your email list
- [ ] **Track analytics** - who's actually engaging/buying?

**If your assumptions don't match reality, UPDATE THIS AVATAR.**
