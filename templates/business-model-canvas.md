# Business Model Canvas

**A one-page strategic tool to map your entire business model**

---

## 📌 What is the Business Model Canvas?

The Business Model Canvas is a visual framework that helps you design, test, and refine your business model on a single page. Created by Alexander Osterwalder, it's used by startups and Fortune 500 companies alike.

---

## 🧩 The 9 Building Blocks

### 1. **Customer Segments** (Who are you serving?)
Define your target customers. Be specific.

**Questions to Answer:**
- Who are your most important customers?
- What are their demographics? (age, location, income, etc.)
- What are their psychographics? (values, beliefs, behaviors)
- Are you serving multiple segments?

**Your Answer:**
```
[Write your customer segments here]

Example: "Aspiring solopreneurs aged 25-40, earning $50K-$100K, 
who feel overwhelmed by business advice and want clear, actionable frameworks."
```

---

### 2. **Value Propositions** (What problem are you solving?)
What makes you different? Why should customers choose you?

**Questions to Answer:**
- What value do you deliver?
- What problem are you solving?
- What needs are you satisfying?
- What bundles of products/services are you offering?

**Your Answer:**
```
[Write your value proposition here]

Example: "We provide free, battle-tested business templates and frameworks 
that eliminate confusion and help entrepreneurs launch faster."
```

---

### 3. **Channels** (How do you reach customers?)
How will you communicate with and deliver value to your customers?

**Types of Channels:**
- **Awareness:** How do they discover you? (SEO, social media, ads, etc.)
- **Evaluation:** How do they evaluate your offer? (website, demos, free trials)
- **Purchase:** How do they buy? (online store, phone, in-person)
- **Delivery:** How do you deliver the product/service?
- **After Sales:** How do you provide support?

**Your Answer:**
```
[List your channels here]

Example:
- Awareness: SEO blog posts, YouTube videos, Twitter threads
- Evaluation: Free templates download, mini-course
- Purchase: Stripe checkout, monthly subscription
- Delivery: Email delivery, member dashboard
- After Sales: Email support, community forum
```

---

### 4. **Customer Relationships** (How do you interact with customers?)
What type of relationship does each customer segment expect?

**Relationship Types:**
- Personal assistance (1-on-1 support)
- Dedicated personal assistance (account managers)
- Self-service (FAQs, knowledge base)
- Automated services (chatbots, email automation)
- Communities (forums, user groups)
- Co-creation (customers help build product)

**Your Answer:**
```
[Describe your customer relationships here]

Example: "Automated onboarding via email sequences, self-service knowledge base, 
community forum for Free users. Pro users get email support. Elite users get 
dedicated 1-on-1 coaching calls."
```

---

### 5. **Revenue Streams** (How do you make money?)
For what value are customers willing to pay?

**Revenue Models:**
- Asset sale (selling ownership)
- Usage fee (pay per use)
- Subscription fee (ongoing access)
- Lending/Renting/Leasing
- Licensing
- Brokerage fees
- Advertising

**Pricing Mechanisms:**
- Fixed pricing (list price, volume-dependent, customer segment-dependent)
- Dynamic pricing (negotiation, yield management, real-time market)

**Your Answer:**
```
[List your revenue streams here]

Example:
- Free tier: $0 (lead generation)
- Pro subscription: $47/month (recurring)
- Elite subscription: $297/month (recurring)
- One-time template packs: $97-$297
- Affiliate commissions: 10-30% on recommended tools
```

---

### 6. **Key Resources** (What assets do you need?)
The most important resources required to make your business model work.

**Types of Resources:**
- **Physical:** facilities, vehicles, machines, systems
- **Intellectual:** brand, patents, copyrights, data, partnerships
- **Human:** team members, skills, expertise
- **Financial:** cash, credit lines, stock options

**Your Answer:**
```
[List your key resources here]

Example:
- Intellectual: Library of 50+ templates, brand reputation, course content
- Human: Founder's expertise, 2 VAs for support, freelance designers
- Financial: $10K runway, Stripe merchant account
- Physical: Website, email infrastructure (ConvertKit), payment processor
```

---

### 7. **Key Activities** (What do you do?)
The most important actions you must take to make your business model work.

**Types of Activities:**
- Production (creating the product/service)
- Problem-solving (consulting, R&D)
- Platform/network (managing communities, matchmaking)

**Your Answer:**
```
[List your key activities here]

Example:
- Creating new templates and courses (10 hours/week)
- Content marketing (blog posts, YouTube videos - 15 hours/week)
- Customer support (5 hours/week)
- Product development and improvement (8 hours/week)
- Marketing and sales (10 hours/week)
```

---

### 8. **Key Partners** (Who helps you?)
The network of suppliers and partners that make your business model work.

**Types of Partnerships:**
- Strategic alliances (non-competitors)
- Strategic partnerships (competitors)
- Joint ventures
- Supplier relationships

**Why Partner?**
- Optimization and economy of scale
- Reduction of risk and uncertainty
- Acquisition of resources and activities

**Your Answer:**
```
[List your key partners here]

Example:
- Stripe (payment processing)
- ConvertKit (email marketing)
- Webflow/GitHub Pages (hosting)
- Canva (design tools)
- Affiliate partners (cross-promotion)
- Freelance writers (content creation)
```

---

### 9. **Cost Structure** (What are your costs?)
All costs incurred to operate your business model.

**Types of Costs:**
- Fixed costs (salaries, rent, utilities)
- Variable costs (materials, commissions)
- Economies of scale
- Economies of scope

**Cost-Driven vs. Value-Driven:**
- **Cost-driven:** Focus on minimizing costs everywhere possible
- **Value-driven:** Focus on creating value, less concerned with cost

**Your Answer:**
```
[List your cost structure here]

Example:
Fixed Costs:
- Website hosting: $20/month
- Email marketing: $79/month
- Software subscriptions: $100/month
- VA support: $500/month

Variable Costs:
- Freelance content: $200-$500/month
- Ads (when running): $500-$2000/month
- Payment processing fees: 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction
- Affiliate commissions: 20% of referred sales
```

## ✅ How to Use This Template

1. **Print it out** or use a digital canvas tool (Miro, Canva, PowerPoint)
2. **Use sticky notes** for each block so you can move ideas around
3. **Start with Customer Segments** - who are you serving?
4. **Define your Value Proposition** - what problem are you solving?
5. **Work through each block** systematically
6. **Iterate and refine** - this is a living document
7. **Test your assumptions** - validate with real customers
8. **Update regularly** - as your business evolves, so should your canvas

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

- **Keep it simple** - one page, clear language
- **Be specific** - "entrepreneurs" is too vague, "solo consultants who bill hourly" is better
- **Focus on what matters** - not everything is equally important
- **Test assumptions** - don't guess, validate with real customers
- **Use it for pivots** - when you pivot, update your canvas
- **Share it with your team** - everyone should understand the business model
- **Revisit quarterly** - markets change, so should your model
