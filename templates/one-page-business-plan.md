**Everything you need to start your business on a single page**

## 🎯 Why One Page?

Traditional business plans are 40+ pages that no one reads. This one-page format forces you to clarify your thinking and focus on what matters most. Perfect for solopreneurs, freelancers, and small business owners.

### 4. **TARGET CUSTOMER** (Who is this for?)

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Print it and put it on your wall** - review weekly
2. **Update it monthly** - as you learn, your plan should evolve
3. **Share it with 3 people** - get feedback before you launch
4. **Test your assumptions** - don't guess, validate
5. **Action > perfection** - a done plan you execute beats a perfect plan you don't
6. **Focus on the bottleneck** - what's the ONE thing stopping you from revenue?
