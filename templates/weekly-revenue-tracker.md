# Weekly Revenue Tracker

**Track your business performance week by week**

---

## 📊 Why Track Weekly?

Monthly tracking is too slow. Daily tracking is too noisy. **Weekly tracking is the sweet spot** - frequent enough to spot trends, stable enough to see signal vs. noise.

---

## 🎯 What to Track

### Core Metrics:
1. **Revenue** - How much money came in
2. **Expenses** - How much money went out
3. **Profit** - Revenue minus expenses
4. **Profit Margin** - Profit divided by revenue (%)

### Growth Metrics:
5. **New Customers** - How many customers you acquired
6. **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)** - Marketing spend ÷ new customers
7. **Lifetime Value (LTV)** - Average revenue per customer over their lifetime
8. **Churn** - How many customers you lost

## 📈 GROWTH FORMULAS

### Key Calculations:

**Profit Margin:**
```
(Profit ÷ Revenue) × 100 = ____%
```

**Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):**
```
Marketing Spend ÷ Number of New Customers = $____
```

**Lifetime Value (LTV):**
```
(Average Order Value × Number of Repeat Purchases) = $____
```

**LTV:CAC Ratio (should be 3:1 or higher):**
```
LTV ÷ CAC = ____:1
```

**Break-Even Point:**
```
Fixed Costs ÷ (Price - Variable Cost per Unit) = ____ units
```

**Runway (months you can survive):**
```
Cash in Bank ÷ Monthly Burn Rate = ____ months
```

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Fill this out every Sunday evening** - make it a ritual
2. **Don't lie to yourself** - accurate data is more important than good-looking data
3. **Look for trends, not single weeks** - one bad week doesn't mean failure
4. **Celebrate wins** - when you hit a goal, acknowledge it
5. **Adjust quickly** - if something isn't working for 3 weeks, change it
6. **Track in a spreadsheet** - copy this to Google Sheets or Excel for automatic calculations
7. **Share with an accountability partner** - transparency drives action

---

## 🚨 Red Flags to Watch For

- **Revenue declining for 3+ weeks** → Investigate immediately
- **Expenses growing faster than revenue** → Cut costs or increase prices
- **Profit margin below 20%** → You're working too hard for too little
- **CAC higher than LTV** → You're losing money on every customer
- **Churn higher than new customers** → You have a retention problem
- **3+ weeks missing targets** → Your goals might be unrealistic OR your strategy needs to change

## ✅ How to Use This Template

1. **Copy to Google Sheets or Excel** for automatic calculations
2. **Set a weekly review time** (Sunday 6pm works for most people)
3. **Gather all data** before you start (bank statements, Stripe dashboard, etc.)
4. **Be honest** - this is for YOU, not investors
5. **Look for patterns** - what's working? What's not?
6. **Take action** - data without action is just numbers
7. **Review monthly and quarterly** - zoom out to see the bigger picture
