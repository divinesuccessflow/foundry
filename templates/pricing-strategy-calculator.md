## 💰 The Pricing Problem

Most entrepreneurs make one of these mistakes:
1. **Price too low** → Attract nightmare clients, work too hard for too little
2. **Price too high** → Scare everyone away, make zero sales
3. **Price randomly** → No strategy, inconsistent results

**The solution?** Use data, psychology, and strategy—not guesswork.

## 🧠 PRICING PSYCHOLOGY HACKS

### 1. **The Decoy Effect**
Add a "decoy" option to make your preferred tier look like the best deal.

**Bad:**
- Basic: $47
- Premium: $497

**Good:**
- Basic: $47
- Pro: $147 ← **BEST VALUE** (most will choose this)
- Elite: $497 (makes Pro look affordable)

### 2. **Anchor High**
Always show the highest price first to anchor expectations.

**Bad:** "$47/month"
**Good:** "Normally $497... Today only $47"

### 3. **Charm Pricing**
Prices ending in 7 or 9 convert better than round numbers.

**Bad:** $50, $100, $500
**Good:** $47, $97, $497

### 4. **Remove the Dollar Sign**
Reduces "pain of paying."

**Bad:** "$497"
**Good:** "497" or "Just 497"

### 5. **Annual Discount**
Offer 2 months free for annual payment.

**Monthly:** $47/month = $564/year
**Annual:** $470/year (save $94 = 2 months free)

## 🚨 PRICE TESTING FRAMEWORK

**Don't guess—test your pricing:**

### Test #1: Start Higher Than You Think

**Why:** It's easier to lower prices than raise them.

**Action:**
1. Launch at $______ (your calculated price)
2. Run for 30 days
3. Track conversion rate: _____%
4. If < 2% conversion → price may be too high OR messaging is off
5. If > 10% conversion → you're probably too cheap

### Test #2: Survey Your Audience

**Ask:**
- "At what price would this be a no-brainer?"
- "At what price would this be expensive but worth considering?"
- "At what price would this be too expensive to consider?"

**Van Westendorp Model:**
- Plot the answers
- Optimal price = where "expensive but worth it" and "cheap" lines cross

### Test #3: A/B Test Pricing Pages

**Version A:** $97
**Version B:** $147

Run equal traffic to both for 2 weeks, compare:
- Conversion rate
- Total revenue
- Choose the winner

## 📈 WHEN TO RAISE YOUR PRICES

**Raise prices when:**

- [ ] You have a waitlist (demand > supply)
- [ ] You're booked solid for 4+ weeks
- [ ] You have social proof (testimonials, case studies)
- [ ] Your conversion rate is > 10%
- [ ] You've improved your offer significantly
- [ ] You want better clients (higher prices = better clients)

**How much to raise:**
- **Small raise:** 10-20% (test, low risk)
- **Medium raise:** 30-50% (repositioning)
- **Large raise:** 100%+ (complete rebrand/tier change)

**Grandfather existing customers** (let them keep old pricing) for goodwill.

## 🚀 REAL-WORLD EXAMPLES

### Example 1: Coaching Business
- **Value Created:** Client increases revenue by $100K
- **Value-Based Price:** $20,000 (20% of value)
- **Cost-Plus Price:** $5,000 (100 hours × $50/hr)
- **Competitive Price:** $8,000 (market average)
- **RECOMMENDED:** $15,000 (split the difference, room to grow)

### Example 2: Digital Product (Course)
- **Cost-Plus:** $500 (100 hours to create × $50/hr ÷ 100 students)
- **Value-Based:** $997 (saves them 50 hours × $100/hr = $5K value → charge 20%)
- **Competitive:** $697 (market average)
- **RECOMMENDED:** $797 (competitive premium, room for sales)

### Example 3: SaaS Product
- **Cost-Plus:** $20/user (hosting, support, dev time)
- **Value-Based:** $100/user (saves 10 hours/month × $50/hr = $500 value → charge 20%)
- **Competitive:** $49/user (market average)
- **RECOMMENDED:** $47/user (charm pricing, competitive)

## 💡 FINAL TIPS

1. **Confidence is everything** - If YOU don't believe your price is fair, no one else will
2. **Never apologize for your price** - You're worth it
3. **Sell the transformation, not the features** - They're not buying coaching, they're buying freedom
4. **Test, test, test** - Your first price is a hypothesis, not a commitment
5. **Raise prices regularly** - Every 6-12 months as you improve
6. **Know your numbers** - Track conversions, revenue, and profit margins religiously
